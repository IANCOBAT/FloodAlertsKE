About this map's coverage

Zone susceptibility for the 38 areas along Nairobi's river corridors follows the 2026 Nairobi Rivers Regeneration Programme mapping (Nairobi East, West, North, Central and South corridors), plus independent flood-exposure research pointing to Kibera, Mathare, Mukuru and Kariobangi as the most consistently affected communities. Twenty-two additional Nairobi areas are included for citywide coverage, scored lower where elevation and drainage are better documented as favourable. Recent context: heavy-rain flooding hit Nairobi in March–May 2024 and again in March–May 2026, in both cases overwhelming the Nairobi and Ngong rivers and disrupting major roads. None of this is a substitute for calibrated hydrological modelling — see "Next production work" in the project README.

This is decision support, not an emergency service. In an active emergency, follow instructions from KMD, Nairobi County and emergency authorities.
